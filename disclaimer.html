私隱政策

收集個人資料聲明

在本網頁以任何線上方式所收集的個人資料，僅為處理使用者的查詢或進行綜合統計。職業訓練局(VTC)不會向第三者洩露使用者的個人識別資料。

私隱政策聲明

職業訓練局承諾遵守《個人資料（私隱）條例》，以符合保護個人資料私隱的國際規定，或更高的標準。為此，VTC會確保員工遵守最嚴格的保安及保密標準。

本網頁只記錄瀏覽者一般資料，不會蒐集可識別瀏覽者身分的個人資料；網上集得的資料將用作進行綜合統計；VTC會遵守《個人資料（私隱）條例》，列明蒐集資料的目的及用途。

如欲讀取更多《個人資料（私隱）條例》資料，請瀏覽網頁 http://www.pco.org.hk/。

注意: 如以上中文譯本與英文文義有異，概以英文文本為準。

Privacy

Personal Information Collection (PIC) Statement

The information about you collected by means of any on-line form at this website will be used only for the purpose of processing your enquiries or compiling aggregate statistics. These information we collect about you will not be disclosed by us to any other party in a form that would identify you.

Privacy Policy Statement (PPS)

Vocational Training Council pledges to meet fully, and where possible exceed, internationally recognised standards of personal data privacy protection, in complying with the requirements of the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. In doing so, we will ensure compliance by our staff with the strictest standards of security and confidentiality.

When you visit our web site we record your visit only as a visit and do not collect any personally identifiable information from you. On-line information collection at this web page is used for include the compilation of aggregate statistics on site usage. We will adhere to the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance that states the purpose and use of the information collected.

For more information of Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, please refer to the following web site, http://www.pco.org.hk/.
